    About Me

    I am a third-year master’s student in Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Zheng Zhang. I received my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai in 2024.

    My research interests lie in AI security, with a focus on trustworthy multimodal foundation models and agents. During my master’s studies, I have primarily worked on adversarial defence for vision-language models (VLMs), aiming to improve their adversarial robustness while preserving their zero-shot generalization.

    I am seeking PhD positions starting in Fall 2027. Please feel free to contact me.
